\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Free-edge scattering of guided waves in a flexoelectric ZnO plate is solved.
\item The backward mode carries 1.93\% of the incident power at the operating point.
\item The share reaches 94.29\% at the zero-group-velocity fold, not as a reflectance.
\item Added carrier-strain-gradient coupling changes the share by 1.2\% to 36\%.
\item Energy budget closes to $10^{-12}$; edge corner force is computed and bounded.
\end{itemize}
