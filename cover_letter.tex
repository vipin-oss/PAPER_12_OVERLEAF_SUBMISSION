\textbf{Submission: Backward-branch negative reflection of guided waves from the free edge of a flexoelectric piezoelectric semiconductor plate}

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for publication in [Journal name].

The manuscript studies what happens when a guided wave reaches the free edge of a flexoelectric piezoelectric semiconductor (n-type ZnO) plate. The model combines strain-gradient elasticity, piezoelectricity, flexoelectricity and linearized electron drift--diffusion, and the edge problem is solved by a spectral Galerkin expansion through the thickness with modal matching at the traction-free edge. The main findings are that the plate supports a backward branch (energy flux opposite to phase velocity) into which the free edge converts part of the incident power; that the backward share is 1.93\,\% at the operating point and reaches 94.29\,\% at the zero-group-velocity fold of the branch, where it is a modal share and not a reflectance; and that the conversion is a property of the backward branch of the plate and not of the added carrier--strain-gradient coupling, which at its tabulated extreme lowers the share by 1.21\,\% at the operating point and by 36.1\,\% at the maximum. The results are checked against exact limits, an independent strong-form integrator, an independent finite-element thickness discretization, a two-dimensional finite-element solution at the operating point, an energy identity, and reciprocity. The assumed values and the limits of the model are stated in the paper.

The manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.
